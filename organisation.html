How organisation partnerships work

A simple three-step process to shape a partnership that supports cleaner streets and practical volunteering.

Step 1

Share your partnership interest

Tell us what your organisation would like to support, from equipment to campaigns or community volunteering.

Open the form
Step 2

Shape the right collaboration

We will have a conversation about your aims, location, audience and the most useful way to work together.

Step 3

Receive the partner support package

Once the partnership is agreed, we provide the practical structure and materials to help the collaboration run smoothly.

Campaign planning

Support shaping a clean-up, awareness drive or local volunteering day around your goals.

Equipment support options

Clear ways to help provide litter pickers, gloves, bags and route materials for communities.

Community activation

Help connecting your support with mosques, students, volunteers and local groups.

Media and design support

Shared graphics, copy and content ideas to help the partnership feel polished.

Impact updates

Simple reporting on volunteers, activity and visible community benefit from the work.
